About us

We are Nythera Games

An independent mobile game studio based in Santiago, Chile.

Who we are

A small studio, on purpose

Nythera is an independent studio: we do not depend on a publisher or investors. We make games for iPhone and Android, from the first idea to the store release.

Being small lets us care for every detail and answer every email ourselves.

Why «Nythera»

The name comes from Nyx, the Greek goddess of the night. We design our games for the end of the day: short, calm sessions to play in bed or on the couch.

Our purpose

Games that respect your time

  • Made to unwind

    Short sessions, no rush and no stressful timers.

  • No accounts

    No sign-ups: your progress stays on your device.

  • Clear about money

    If a game has ads, we say where they appear and how to remove them.

  • In your language

    We publish in Spanish, English and Portuguese.
