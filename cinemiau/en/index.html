How it plays

One premiere for every puzzle

  • Sort colors

    A stone sorting puzzle that is easy to learn and hard to put down.

  • Finish the pixel art

    Big pixel art boards with an optional automatic hint.

  • Fill your poster wall

    Every finished level flips the card and adds a premiere to your wall.

  • Play your way

    Free, no accounts, and it works offline. Ads start at level 9 and a one-time purchase removes them.
