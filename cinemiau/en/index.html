How it plays

One premiere for every puzzle

  1. 01

    Sort by color

    Tap a group of stones of the same color, keep it in the reserve if there's no room yet, and drop it into its spots.

  2. 02

    Finish the pixel art

    The board builds up like a mosaic. When the last stone lands, the artwork is revealed and the title appears.

  3. 03

    Reveal the poster

    The card flips to show the movie poster: an original parody starring cats.

  4. 04

    Fill your poster wall

    Every premiere is saved in Now showing. Over 100 movies, from horror to sci-fi.

Play your way

  • Free with no accounts: your progress stays on your device.
  • Works offline.
  • Ads start at level 9 and a one-time purchase removes them.
  • Hints when you get stuck, and relaxing music you can mute.
