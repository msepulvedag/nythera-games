Cinemiau: Cat Puzzle

Sort colored stones, finish the pixel art and flip the card to reveal a movie poster starring… cats!

  • A color sort puzzle that is easy to learn and hard to put down.
  • Big pixel art boards with an optional automatic hint.
  • A poster wall that fills with premieres as you play.
  • No ads, no in-app purchases, no accounts. Works offline.
