Cinemiau Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026 · Español · English · Português

Controller: Nythera Games. Contact: contacto@nythera.games.

In short

Cinemiau has no accounts or servers of its own and does not collect personal data for itself. To stay free it shows ads from Google AdMob, which may process certain device data according to your consent choices. You can remove ads with a one-time purchase.

Data stored on your device

  • Progress and settings (completed levels, sound, music, vibration, hints, language, whether you saw the tutorial, your consent answers and whether you bought “No Ads”): stored only on your device and deleted if you uninstall the app.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

  • From level 9 (after the two-board tutorial) the app shows Google AdMob ads: a banner on the game screen, occasional full-screen ads and optional rewarded ads to enlarge the tray.
  • Google, as an independent controller, may collect and process the device advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS, Advertising ID on Android), the IP address and approximate location derived from it, device and system information, and ad interactions, to serve, measure and personalize ads and to prevent fraud.
  • Your choice: in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland and the U.S. states that require it, we ask for consent with Google’s tool (UMP). On iOS, Apple asks whether you allow tracking; if you don’t, ads are not personalized with the IDFA. You can change this in your device’s privacy settings.
  • More information: how Google uses data from apps that use its services and Google’s privacy policy.

“No Ads” purchase

Purchases are processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We never receive your payment details; the app only receives confirmation that the purchase exists in order to remove ads.

Permissions

The app uses the internet only for ads and purchases. It does not request access to the camera, microphone, precise location, contacts, photos or files.

Children

Cinemiau is for a general audience and is not specifically directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children; if you believe a child has sent us data, contact us and we will delete it.

Your rights

Because we keep no personal data on our own servers, we hold no data about you to provide or delete; your progress is deleted when you uninstall. For data processed by Google for ads, you can exercise your rights with Google and reset or limit your advertising identifier in your device settings.

Changes to this policy

If we change how data is handled, we will update this page and the date above.

Contact

Write to contacto@nythera.games.
