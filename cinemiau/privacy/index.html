Cinemiau Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 6, 2026 · Español

Controller: Nythera Games. Contact: contacto@nythera.games.

In short

Cinemiau does not collect, store on servers or share personal data. It has no accounts, ads, purchases, or third-party analytics or advertising tools.

What the app does with your information

  • Progress and settings (completed levels, sound, music, vibration, automatic hint, tutorial seen): stored only on your device. Uninstalling the app deletes them.
  • Internet connection: the app neither uses nor requests it. It works offline.
  • Permissions: it does not request access to the camera, microphone, location, contacts, photos or files.
  • Advertising identifiers: not used.
  • Third-party services: the app includes no advertising, analytics or social SDKs. The stores (Google Play and the App Store) may process download data under their own policies.

Children

Cinemiau is not specifically directed at children under 13 and does not collect data from anyone, including minors.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever adds features that process data (for example, online backups), we will update this policy and announce it on the store listing before the update.

Contact

Email contacto@nythera.games with any privacy question.
