Privacy Policy — Fireflies: The Last Light

Last updated: November 27, 2026

Nythera Games ("we") develops the game Fireflies: The Last Light ("the game") for iOS and Android. This policy explains what data is processed when you play and why.

What we store

The game has no user accounts and does not ask for your name, email or any personal data. Your progress (levels, stars, hints) and settings (sound, music, language, "No ads" purchase) are stored only on your device and are deleted when you uninstall the game.

Third-party services

The game uses these services, which may collect data from your device under their own policies:

  • Ads (Unity LevelPlay, Unity Ads and Google AdMob) — from version 1.0.1, from level 21 on: Unity LevelPlay (ironSource) routes ad requests to networks such as Unity Ads and Google AdMob. These services may use your advertising identifier, IP address, approximate location derived from the IP address, technical device data (model, language, screen, connection type), usage and performance data and ad interactions to show, cap and measure ads and to prevent fraud. The game does not use precise location (GPS). Unity ads include a privacy icon with your options. Policies: Unity · Google. A banner appears at the bottom while you solve a level; there are occasional full-screen ads and optional rewarded ads.
  • Google Analytics for Firebase (usage statistics): records anonymous gameplay events (for example "level started", "level completed", "hint used"), an installation identifier and technical device data, to understand how the game is played and improve it. Google SDKs (AdMob and Firebase) may collect error and performance data, such as crash logs, diagnostics, and app stability and performance data. Policy: Firebase.
  • App Store / Google Play ("No ads" purchase): payment is processed by the store; we never receive your payment details.

We do not sell your data for money. Advertising partners use it for ads as described above.

Your consent and choices

  • Europe, the UK and other regions with privacy laws: where required, the game may ask for your consent with a Google form before showing ads. If it appears, you can change your choice in Settings › Ad privacy.
  • iOS: the game asks whether you allow tracking (App Tracking Transparency). If you don't, ad networks cannot access your iPhone's advertising identifier (IDFA). You can change it in iPhone Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.
  • Android: you can reset or delete your advertising ID in phone Settings › Google › Ads.
  • The "No ads" purchase removes all ads from the game.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages but is not specifically directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided us with data, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will publish the new version on this page with its date.

Permissions

The game does not request access to your camera, microphone, location, contacts, photos or files.
