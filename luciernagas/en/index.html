Fireflies: The Last Light

The forest has gone dark. Only one firefly is left… and you. Connect every firefly in one stroke to bring light back to the forest.

How it plays

  1. Connect the fireflies

    Draw one continuous line through every firefly on the board without lifting your finger.

  2. Visit each spot once

    Draw your path without passing through the same spot twice.

  3. Light up the forest

    Every puzzle you solve lights up more of the forest. If you get stuck, a hint marks the start and shows part of the path.

The story

The Last Light in the Forest

Ten illustrated scenes light up as you play, with original music and narration in English and Spanish. The text is highlighted word by word.

Play at your own pace

  • 100 levels of growing difficulty and a short tutorial.
  • No timers or lives. Replay levels to improve your stars.
  • Earn three stars by solving without restarting and win extra hints with your streaks.
  • Adjust effects, music, vibration and narration.
